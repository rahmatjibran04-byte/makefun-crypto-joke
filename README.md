One line. One joke. Every trader has lived it.

> I bought the dip. It dipped again. Now it's a long-term investment — because selling means admitting I was wrong.

The angle: "buy the dip" is the most repeated advice in crypto, and every trader knows the follow-up nobody says out loud — the dip keeps dipping, and the bag quietly gets rebranded as a "long-term investment". The punchline lands at the very end and names the real reason nobody sells: not strategy, not conviction — pride. At 113 characters the joke stays tight enough that every word earns its place, which is exactly what the tie-break rewards.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the joke to `/output/entry.txt` (one line, at most 200 characters, validated before writing).
